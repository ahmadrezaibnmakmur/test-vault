# AnyFlo ID Strategy

## Core Idea

AnyFlo.id adalah flexible workflow platform yang memungkinkan perusahaan membangun proses operasional berdasarkan cara kerja mereka sendiri, bukan berdasarkan logika aplikasi yang kaku.

Positioning seed:

> Your workflow, your logic, your system.

Simpler tagline candidate:

> Your workflow. Your rules.

## Problem Statement

Banyak perusahaan dipaksa mengikuti alur aplikasi yang kaku, padahal proses kerja mereka unik, berubah-ubah, dan membutuhkan fleksibilitas tinggi.

Common pain points:

- Approval flow tidak bisa disesuaikan.
- Form dan field terbatas.
- Proses harus mengikuti sistem, bukan sebaliknya.
- Request perubahan ke vendor lambat dan mahal.
- Data tersebar di spreadsheet, WhatsApp, email, dan aplikasi berbeda.
- Tim operasional sulit tracking status pekerjaan.
- Manajemen tidak punya visibility real-time.

## Value Proposition

AnyFlo.id membantu perusahaan mendigitalisasi workflow sesuai proses kerja mereka sendiri, tanpa harus mengikuti logika aplikasi yang kaku.

Sharp versions:

- Custom workflow without custom software complexity.
- Flexible workflow system for companies with unique operational processes.
- AnyFlo.id helps companies turn messy manual processes into flexible digital workflows that match the way they actually work.

## Positioning Statement

For growing companies with unique and complex operational processes, AnyFlo.id is a flexible workflow platform that allows teams to design, run, and monitor custom workflows without being forced to follow rigid software logic.

## Differentiation

| Alternative | Weakness | AnyFlo.id Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Spreadsheet | Flexible but messy | More structured, trackable, scalable |
| Standard SaaS | Neat but rigid | Adjustable to company process |
| Custom software | Flexible but expensive and slow | Faster and more affordable |
| No-code tools | Powerful but requires maker skill | Packaged as ready workflow solution |
| Large ERP | Complete but heavy | Lighter for department-level workflows |

Differentiation statement:

> AnyFlo.id combines the flexibility of spreadsheets, the structure of software, and the speed of no-code implementation.

## Messaging Framework

Main message:

> Stop forcing your team to follow rigid software. Build workflows that follow how your company actually works.

Indonesian version:

> Berhenti memaksa tim mengikuti aplikasi yang kaku. Bangun workflow yang mengikuti cara kerja perusahaan Anda.

Outcome message:

Dengan AnyFlo.id, perusahaan bisa mempercepat approval, mengurangi pekerjaan manual, meningkatkan visibility, dan memastikan setiap proses berjalan sesuai SOP.
