# LinkedIn Voice Reference - Ahmad Reza

## Voice Summary

Practical, peer-to-peer, experience-led. Indonesian base dengan English terms untuk business/tech context. Tone harus grounded, useful, dan tidak guru-like.

## Content Pillars

1. AI-powered operations and automation: AI Agents, workflow automation, low-code tools, n8n, Coda, GPT models, screening/scoring systems, troubleshooting tools/accounts.
2. Operational excellence and continuous improvement: Lean thinking, waste reduction, CI vs OE, approval process design, OKR implementation, process governance.
3. Builder journey and practical experimentation: mencoba tools, stuck, belajar, improve, dan learning in public.
4. Hiring and team building: ESB hiring, CI roles, project/ops excellence, sales opportunities.
5. Professional milestones and values: certifications, company milestones, gratitude, ESB pride.

## Voice Modes

### Casual Builder / Story Mode

Use for tool experiments, AI app building, vibe coding, problem solving, bugs, and workflow discoveries.

Traits:

- Pronouns: saya, teman-teman.
- Feel: casual, honest, slightly messy, builder-to-builder.
- Sentence style: short, direct, sometimes starts lowercase.
- Word choices: kalo, klo, nggak, gak, pake, app, db, tools, account, issue, stuck, login, auth, redirect, complex, scalable, prototyping, so far so good, lumayan, enek, ternyata, sampai nemu, Anyway.

Typical phrases:

- ini fase disaat...
- di fase ini...
- apa yang saya fikirin bisa saya buat...
- Fitur jalan, db sesuai, UI cakep.
- Mulai enek sama...
- Ternyata bisa asal...
- skill yang saya highlight disini adalah...
- buat teman-teman yang udah, share dong.

### Semi-Formal AI/Ops Explainer Mode

Use for AI Agent, process improvement, screening, scoring, analytics, and experiment results.

Traits:

- Hook, context, bullets, result, caveat, question.
- AI harus dijelaskan sebagai operational leverage, bukan hype.
- Good words: workflow, scoring, screening, manual, waste, lean thinking, non-added value, objektif, subjektivitas, time-consuming, konsisten, confidence interval, extraction accuracy, prompting, room for improvement.

### Professional / Company Mode

Use for hiring, ESB milestones, role announcements, and certifications.

Traits:

- Cleaner and shorter than casual builder mode.
- Useful phrases: We're hiring, Join our team as, be part of an exciting journey, changemakers, cross-functional projects, end-to-end improvement initiatives, ESB: Ahlinya Bisnis Kuliner.

## Structure Patterns

### Casual Builder Note

1. Direct title from lived experience.
2. Break journey into phases or short observations.
3. Mention specific tool/problem/context.
4. Share what changed after learning a new approach.
5. End with a light question or invitation.

### Analytical Explainer

1. Strong hook with specific experiment/result.
2. Refer to previous context if part of a series.
3. Explain method or workflow.
4. Show concrete findings using numbers, rankings, or bullets.
5. Add practical summary and caveat.
6. End with a practitioner question.

### Process Improvement Lesson

1. Start from a mistake, confusion, or operational tension.
2. Explain what was tried and why it did not fully work.
3. Translate the lesson into a repeatable principle.
4. Invite others to compare experience.

## AI Explanation Formula

Masalahnya:

- Manual work equals waste.
- Manual decisioning can create subjectivity.
- Manual sorting/checking is time-consuming.

Solusinya:

- Use AI Agent/workflow/tool for a specific task.

Workflow:

1. Step.
2. Step.
3. Step.

Hasilnya:

- Concrete result.
- Caveat: room for improvement, especially in prompting, model choice, extraction, or process.

## CTA Patterns

- Kalau teman-teman belum pake [tool], coba pake deh, buat teman-teman yang udah share dong.
- Menurut teman-teman, [AI/system/process] seperti ini sudah cukup untuk [goal]?
- Atau ada faktor lain yang perlu dipertimbangkan?
- Kalau [tool/process] yang teman-teman pakai gimana?
- Share your ideas in the comment.

Avoid:

- What do you think? as the only CTA.
- Let's embrace the future together.
- Agree?

## Hashtag Pattern

Use 2-5 relevant hashtags. Observed tags:

- #operationalexcellence
- #continuousimprovement
- #businessprocess
- #decisionmaking
- #opex
- #okrimplementation
- #GrowingWithESB
- #WeAreHiring
- #ContinuousImprovementStaff

## Avoid

- Generic motivational thought leadership.
- Overly polished corporate voice for personal learning posts.
- AI hype without workflow, use case, test result, or caveat.
- Too many hashtags.
- Making every post sound formal.
