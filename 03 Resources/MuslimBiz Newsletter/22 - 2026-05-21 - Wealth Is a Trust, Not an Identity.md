# Wealth Is a Trust, Not an Identity

As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
Wealth Is a
Trust
, Not an Identity
Wealth was never meant to become a Muslim’s identity.
Today, many people measure success through symbols:
the car
the house
the office
the watch
the neighborhood
The Wrong
Standard
of Success
A man can pray at the masjid every day, earn halal income, stay away from riba, take care of his parents, raise righteous children, give in charity, and protect his family from haram...
Yet if he drives an older car or lives modestly, society quietly treats him as if he “didn’t make it.”
People feel bad for him.
They think he needs help.
Meanwhile another man may be drowning in debt, arrogance, showing off, neglecting his family and deen, but because he displays wealth, people immediately respect him.
When in reality, the latter needs help far more than the former.
When Wealth
Changes
Perception
This disease has entered even parts of the Muslim community.
A brother mentioned that an imam he had asked to go out for coffee had barely given him attention and rejected him. The interaction was cold and dismissive.
Then one day the imam visited the brother’s office through another friend and saw the scale of his business, his setup, his status, and suddenly his attitude completely changed.
The brother himself did not change.
His character did not change.
His taqwa did not change.
Only the perception of his wealth changed.
Wealth Is an
Amanah
This is dangerous because Islam teaches us that wealth is an amanah from Allah سبحانه وتعالى, not a measure of human value.
The Sahabah رضي الله عنهم respected people for:
deen
sacrifice
honesty
knowledge
courage
character
Not for leather seats and square footage.
Own
Wealth
. Do Not Let It Own You.
Money can help a Muslim do enormous good.
But once wealth becomes identity instead of
responsibility, the
soul begins to rot while the dunya applauds.
A Muslim should own wealth.
Wealth should never own the Muslim.
A Different Standard
How often have you encountered situations like this in your community?
