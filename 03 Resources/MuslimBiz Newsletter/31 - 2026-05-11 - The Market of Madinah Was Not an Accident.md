As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
The Market of Madinah Was Not an
Accident
The Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم did not only establish a Masjid.
He established a market.
Because Allah سبحانه وتعالى was teaching this Ummah that true independence starts with economic independence.
Markets
Shape
Power
Today, markets are no longer only physical.
Social media platforms are markets.
Amazon is a market.
App stores are markets.
Stock exchanges are markets.
Search engines are markets.
AI platforms are markets.
And Muslims own almost none of them.
Instead, we live inside other people’s systems:
their platforms
their rules
their algorithms
their payment systems
their infrastructure
their values
Whoever
Controls
the Market Controls Influence
Historically, whoever controlled the markets controlled power.
And today, digital markets shape:
wealth
influence
culture
information
even how people think
This is why Muslims cannot remain satisfied with being only users and consumers.
The Ummah
Needs
Builders Again
The Ummah needs builders again.
Builders of platforms.
Builders of software.
Builders of marketplaces.
Builders of systems rooted in halal foundations from the beginning.
Because true economic independence is not asking existing systems to remove the haram.
It is building alternatives with halal foundations from the start.
A Different
Vision
for the Ummah
What industries would Muslims dominate today if we viewed building marketplaces as part of serving the Ummah?
