# The Richest People May Come Empty-Handed

As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
The
Richest
People May Come Empty-Handed
Many Muslims see a wealthy Muslim and immediately say, "Allah has blessed him with barakah."
Perhaps.
But barakah is not what you see.
Barakah is what you do not see.
Barakah
Is More Than Wealth
Barakah is whether that wealth brought a person closer to Allah سبحانه وتعالى or further away.
Barakah is whether the money was earned through halal means when haram opportunities were available.
Barakah is whether the wealth funded masajid, students of knowledge, families, and charitable causes, or whether it was spent on excess, status, and desires.
Barakah is whether that money helped a person wake up for Fajr, perform Hajj, support his parents, and strengthen the Ummah, or whether it distracted him from the very purpose for which he was created.
Wealth and
Success
Are Not the Same
A person can have millions in the bank and be bankrupt in the akhirah.
Another can have far less wealth yet arrive before Allah سبحانه وتعالى carrying mountains of good deeds because he earned and spent what he had correctly.
The value of wealth is not determined by how much exists.
It is determined by what it produces.
Wealth Will Be
Questioned
Twice
The Messenger of Allah صلى الله عليه وسلم said:
"The feet of the son of Adam shall not move from before his Lord on the Day of Resurrection until he is asked about five things: about his life and what he did with it, about his youth and how he spent it, about his wealth and how he earned it and spent it, and what he did with what he knew."
(Sunan At-Tirmidhi 2417, Hasan)
Notice that wealth will be questioned twice.
How it was earned
How it was spent
That alone should make every Muslim think differently about success.
The Real
Measure
of Wealth
The true measure of wealth is not what enters your account.
It is what reaches your scale of good deeds.
Money is a tool.
It can become a source of reward that continues after death, or a burden that follows a person into the Hereafter.
The choice is made long before the questioning begins.
A Different Definition of Rich
What would change in your life if you evaluated every financial decision by the answer you would give on the Day of Judgment?
