As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
Shukr Is Not
Optional
Most Muslim founders are obsessed with growth… but they ignore the one thing that actually causes it.
As a business owner, you are always trying to sell more, increase revenue, refine strategy, and build something bigger.
All of that is good.
But there is something deeper that most people miss.
The Hidden Factor Behind
Growth
The attitude you carry while building can either unlock increase… or block it.
Two founders can put in the same effort.
One sees barakah, expansion, and unexpected openings.
The other stays stuck, even while working harder.
The difference is not always strategy.
It is shukr.
The Promise and the
Warning
Allah سبحانه وتعالى says:
“And (remember) when your Lord proclaimed: ‘If you give thanks (by accepting Faith and worshipping none but Allâh), I will give you more (of My Blessings); but if you are thankless (i.e. disbelievers), verily My punishment is indeed severe.’”
(Qur’an 14:7)
This ayah comes with a double edge.
There is no neutral position.
No Neutral
Ground
Either you are grateful… and you are increased.
Or you are ungrateful… and something is taken away.
Not being thankful is not a passive state.
It is a dangerous one.
The Real
Decision
You Are Making
So the Muslim founder is not just choosing between
growth strategies.
He is choosing between two paths:
Increase… or loss.
And that choice is not made in your business plan.
It is made in your heart.
Anchor Your Work in Shukr
So you keep building.
You keep pushing.
You keep executing.
But you anchor all of it in shukr.
Because rizq was never just about effort.
It was always about your relationship with Allah سبحانه وتعالى, the One who provides it.
So ask yourself:
What would change in the way you build if you truly believed that your next level of growth is tied to how grateful you are at your current level?
