# Rizq Does Not Increase Through Panic

As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
Rizq Does Not
Increase
Through Panic
One of the clearest signs that a person has forgotten who controls provision is that he lives in constant panic.
A delayed customer payment.
A slow sales month.
A customer complaint.
He panics.
And suddenly ev
eryone around him suffers.
Panic
Spreads
to Everyone Around You
His employees feel it.
His vendors feel it.
His family and community feel it.
He becomes impatient, harsh, reactive, and emotionally unstable, as if panic itself will solve the problem or increase his rizq.
But panic does not increase provision.
It usually makes the situation worse.
Panic Weakens
Judgment
Because a panicked person cannot think clearly.
He cannot make balanced decisions.
He cannot lead properly.
He cannot focus in salah.
He cannot calmly make duʿā’ to Allah سبحانه وتعالى for guidance.
Allah سبحانه وتعالى described the righteous servants:
“And the slaves of the Most Gracious (Allah) are those who walk on the earth in humility and sedateness...”
(Surah Al-Furqan 25:63)
The Muslim walks with sakinah.
Not with panic.
Calmness
Is Strength
The Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم also said:
“Deliberateness is from Allah, and haste is from Shaytan.”
(Sunan al-Tirmidhī 2012)
This does not mean Muslims become lazy.
It means they combine disciplined effort with trust in Allah سبحانه وتعالى.
They work hard.
They prepare.
They plan.
They act.
But they do not emotionally collapse every time something goes wrong.
What Is
Written
Will Reach You
Because they understand:
What Allah سبحانه وتعالى wrote for them will reach them.
No panic can increase it.
No fear can protect what was never meant to stay.
A Different Business Culture
What would change in Muslim businesses if calmness replaced panic?
