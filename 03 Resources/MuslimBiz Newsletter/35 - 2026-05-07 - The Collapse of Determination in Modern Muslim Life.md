# The Collapse of Determination in Modern Muslim Life

As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
The Collapse of
Determination
in Modern Muslim Life
One of the greatest plagues of the modern Muslim generation is weak determination.
A person starts a degree, then changes it five times.
Starts a business and quits.
Starts practicing seriously, then disappears after a few months.
A person who cannot remain firm eventually becomes weak in every area of life.
Islam builds people of resolve and determination.
The People of
Strong
Will
Allah سبحانه وتعالى praised the Prophets as:
“Therefore be patient (O Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم) as did the Messengers of strong will...”
(Surah Al-Ahqaf 46:35)
This is the model.
Not people who begin everything and complete nothing.
But people who remain firm through hardship, delay, and sacrifice.
Determination Begins with
Tawhid
So how does a Muslim develop determination?
First, strengthen tawhid and certainty in Allah سبحانه وتعالى.
Most hesitation comes from:
fear of rizq
fear of failure
fear of people
fear of uncertainty
attachment to dunya
But when a Muslim truly believes that rizq, outcomes, and honor are all from Allah سبحانه وتعالى, courage begins to grow.
Discipline
Creates Resolve
Second, build discipline through small, consistent wins.
The Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم said:
“The most beloved deeds to Allah are those done consistently, even if small.”
(Sahih al-Bukhari)
Determination is built through consistency:
waking up on time
praying on time
finishing responsibilities even when emotions disappear
Your
Environment
Shapes Your Strength
Third, keep company with strong people.
Determination spreads.
So does weakness.
The Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم said:
“A man follows the religion of his close friend, so let one of you look at whom he befriends.”
(Sunan Abi Dawud)
Fourth, learn to finish things.
Many people love beginnings.
Very few reach completion.
The determined Muslim becomes known for one thing:
“When he starts, he finishes.”
Sins Weaken the Will
Fifth, reduce sins.
Ibn al-Qayyim رحمه الله explained that sins weaken:
willpower
clarity
discipline
courage
A person addicted to desires rarely develops strong resolve.
Determination Is Built Through Struggle
Determination is not built in comfort.
It is built through obedience, discipline, sacrifice, and tawakkul in Allah سبحانه وتعالى.
What would happen to the Ummah if Muslims became known again for firmness, endurance, and completion instead of distraction and quitting?
