# What Will Be Working When You Are Gone?

As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
What Will Be Working When You Are
Gone
?
A Muslim’s life does not end when his body is buried.
It either continues… or it stops completely.
There is no middle ground.
Legacy
Is Not Optional
Legacy is not a luxury in Islam.
It is an outcome of how you live, earn, and build.
The Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم said:
“When a man dies, his deeds come to an end except for three things: Sadaqah Jariyah (continuous charity), knowledge by which people benefit, or a righteous child who prays for him.”
(Sahih Muslim)
If you look closely, these are not random categories.
They are three systems of continuity.
Economic continuity.
Intellectual continuity.
Lineage continuity.
Building Economic
Continuity
Sadaqah Jariyah requires capacity.
You cannot give continuously if you do not have.
That means legacy begins with economic independence.
Building something that produces beyond your needs.
Building
Intellectual
Continuity
Beneficial knowledge requires depth.
You cannot pass on what you never took seriously.
That means legacy requires learning first.
Discipline before speech.
Substance before influence.
Building
Lineage Continuity
A righteous child requires intentional upbringing.
Children do not become righteous by default.
They require guidance, structure, and nurturing.
That means legacy requires time, effort, and presence.
You raise them upon Islam, then they carry you after death.
Legacy Is Structured, Not Emotional
S
o legacy in Islam is not emotional.
It is structured.
You build wealth so you can give.
You seek knowledge so you can teach.
You raise children so they can continue.
All three are long-term commitments.
All three require sacrifice.
All three outlive you.
The Real Goal
Most people want to be remembered.
But Islam is not asking you to be remembered.
It is asking you to continue benefiting after you are gone.
That only happens when your life is designed beyond your lifetime.
