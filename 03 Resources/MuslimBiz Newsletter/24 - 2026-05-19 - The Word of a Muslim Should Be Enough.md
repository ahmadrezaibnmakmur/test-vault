# The Word of a Muslim Should Be Enough

As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
The Word of a Muslim
Should
Be Enough
Modern people treat commitment like a temporary emotion.
The first better opportunity appears, and promises disappear.
But one of the most powerful lessons on loyalty, contracts, and commitment comes from the story of Musa عليه السلام.
A
Commitment
Built on Trust
After fleeing Egypt with nothing, exhausted and in desperate need, Musa عليه السلام was offered safety, marriage, and work by the righteous man of Madyan.
Allah سبحانه وتعالى says:
“He said: ‘I intend to wed one of these two daughters of mine to you, on condition that you serve me for eight years; but if you complete ten years, it will be your own choice…’” (Surah Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:27)
This was not a two-week trial period.
Not a temporary arrangement until something “better” appeared.
It was an eight-year commitment with two additional optional years.
Musa عليه السلام
Fulfilled
His Word
And Musa عليه السلام fulfilled it.
Allah سبحانه وتعالى says:
“Then, when Musa had fulfilled the term…”
(Surah Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:29)
The scholars mention that Musa عليه السلام completed the better and longer term.
The Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم said:
“Musa fulfilled the more complete and better of the two terms.” (Musnad Ahmad)
Commitment
Is Part of Noble Character
This shows something many people today no longer understand:
Long-term contracts, commitments, and stability are not against Islam.
They are part of honorable character.
Even more powerful is this:
There was no lawyer.
No legal department.
No written contract.
It was a verbal agreement between two believers.
When Words
Lose
Their Value
Today, many Muslims sign agreements and still betray them.
Others give verbal commitments and break them the moment a slightly better opportunity appears.
But the word of a Muslim was historically treated as something sacred.
A promise carried weight because character carried weight.
Allah سبحانه وتعالى Was Building a Prophet
Musa عليه السلام was not preparing to become an ordinary man.
Allah سبحانه وتعالى was building the character of a Prophet through:
responsibility
patience
loyalty
fulfilling agreements over long periods of time
A generation that cannot stay committed will struggle to build families, businesses, institutions, or civilizations.
A Forgotten Standard
What would change in the Muslim world if loyalty and honoring agreements once again became part of our identity instead of optional behavior?
