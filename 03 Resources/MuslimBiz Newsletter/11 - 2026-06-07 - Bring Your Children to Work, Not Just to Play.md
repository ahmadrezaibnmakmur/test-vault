# Bring Your Children to Work, Not Just to Play

As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
Bring Your Children to
Work
, Not Just to Play
Many parents hope that one day their children will take responsibility in their business or even work.
The problem is that "one day" rarely arrives.
School becomes the priority.
Then sports.
Then extracurricular activities.
Then summer arrives, and after months of studying, the children are tired. We tell ourselves we'll start next summer.
Then another summer passes.
Before we realize it, our children graduate with years of classroom education but almost no real-world responsibility.
Treat Their
Development
Seriously
Even when we do bring our children into the business, we often fail to treat their development seriously.
A new employee receives onboarding, training, goals, feedback, accountability, and performance reviews.
Our own children often receive none of these.
We are lenient to the point of being neglectful.
This is a mistake.
Responsibility
Comes From Real Work
There is no school, university, or extracurricular activity that teaches responsibility like real work.
Real work teaches accountability, discipline, communication, problem solving, and the relationship between effort and results.
Most parents spend sixteen or more years preparing their children to find a job using standard schooling and extracurricular activities.
Far fewer spend sixteen years preparing them to perform a job, create value, solve problems, serve customers, lead people, or build something of their own.
More Than
Wealth
A degree may help a child enter the workforce.
Responsibility prepares them to succeed in it.
The Messenger of Allah صلى الله عليه وسلم said:
"It is better that you leave your inheritors wealthy than leave them poor, begging from others."
(Sahih al-Bukhari 5354)
If a person is just handed over wealth and he doesn't know how to manage it, it will go as it came.
Part of the wealth needed to give to our children is the discipline, skills, character, and judgment required to preserve and grow what they inherit.
The Responsibilities They Will Carry
What responsibilities are you intentionally preparing your children to carry ten years from now?
