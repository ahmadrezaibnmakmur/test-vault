# A Business Is Only Successful If It Pleases Allah

As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
A Business Is Only Successful If It
Pleases
Allah
A business can be growing and still be failing.
Revenue is increasing.
Customers are coming.
Profits are rising.
People are talking about your company.
You feel proud of what you have built.
From the outside, everything looks successful.
But then there is a small compromise here.
A questionable transaction there.
A marketing tactic you know is not quite right.
A business practice you wish was different.
An obligation that keeps getting delayed because the business is too busy.
Over time, these things become easy to justify.
After all, the business is growing.
How Success Is
Measured
Modern business culture teaches us to measure success through revenue, profit, valuation, market share, and followers.
Islam begins with a different question.
Is Allah (سبحانه وتعالى) pleased with what you are building?
If the answer is no, then it does not matter how fast the business is growing.
It does not matter how high the profits are.
Success
Beyond
Profit
Because success is not defined by the market.
Success is defined by obedience to Allah (سبحانه وتعالى).
A business can gain customers while losing barakah.
It can gain wealth while accumulating sins.
The goal for the Muslim is to earn halal, benefit creation, and return to Allah (سبحانه وتعالى) with a clean record.
The
Question
Every Founder Must Answer
The Messenger of Allah (صلى الله عليه وسلم) said:
"The son of Adam will not be dismissed from before his Lord on the Day of Resurrection until he has been questioned about... his wealth and how he earned it and how he disposed of it."
(Tirmidhi 2422)
The market rewards results.
Allah (سبحانه وتعالى) rewards obedience.
What would change in your business if pleasing Allah (سبحانه وتعالى) became the primary measure of success rather than growth alone?
