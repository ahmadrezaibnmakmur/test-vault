# Ambition Builds. Greed Destroys.

As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
Ambition Builds.
Greed
Destroys.
One of the characteristics that successful business people often develop is ambition.
The desire to grow, improve, build something meaningful and serve more people.
Islam does not condemn ambition. In fact, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ encouraged Muslims to aim high.
He said:
"When you ask Allah, ask Him for Al-Firdaws..."
(Sahih al-Bukhari)
Likewise, Allah loves excellence. The Prophet ﷺ said:
"Indeed, Allah loves that when one of you performs a deed, he perfects it."
(Al-Bayhaqi, authenticated by Al-Albani رحمه الله)
The Muslim is not meant to be lazy, passive, or content with mediocrity.
He aims high, works hard, and strives for excellence.
When
Ambition
Changes
But ambition carries a hidden danger.
What begins as a sincere desire to build can slowly become a desire to possess.
A founder wants growth.
Then more growth.
Then market dominance.
Then complete control.
Then another business.
Then another acquisition.
Then another victory.
The
Nature
of the Human Soul
The Prophet ﷺ warned us about the nature of the human soul:
"If the son of Adam had a valley full of gold, he would like to have two valleys, for nothing fills his mouth except dust, and Allah accepts the repentance of whoever repents."
(Sahih al-Bukhari 6436, Sahih Muslim 1048)
Greed is dangerous because it can never be satisfied.
The target keeps moving.
The numbers are never enough.
The wealth is never enough.
Beware of
Greed
The Prophet ﷺ also said:
"Beware of greed, for greed destroyed those who came before you. It commanded them to be stingy, so they were stingy; it commanded them to sever ties, so they severed them; and it commanded them to commit sins, so they committed sins."
(Sunan Abi Dawud 1698, Sahih)
A Question for Every Founder
The Muslim founder must constantly ask himself:
Am I building for Allah, or have I slowly started worshipping growth itself?
