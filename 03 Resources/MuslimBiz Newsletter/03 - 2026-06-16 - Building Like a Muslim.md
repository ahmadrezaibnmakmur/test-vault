# Building Like a Muslim

As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
Building Like a
Muslim
One of the strange things happening today is that many Muslims think rebuilding the Ummah means copying whatever already exists.
So we end up with halal burgers.
Halal cola.
Halal pizza.
Halal versions of products that were never designed around Islamic values in the first place.
I remember hearing about a “Halal Car Wash” in Albania.
Nobody knew what made it halal.
People were simply expected to support it because it had the label.
Muslim Leadership Was Built on
Originality
But Muslim leadership was never built on imitation.
It was built on originality.
The Prophet ﷺ did not establish a Muslim version of every market that already existed. He established a market in Madinah that reflected Islamic principles and solved real problems for Muslims.
That is a very different mindset.
The Muslim founder should not begin by asking:
“What successful business can I copy?”
Start With the
Problem
He should begin by asking:
“What problem exists that needs solving?”
Then he should ask:
“Can I solve it in a way that pleases Allah سبحانه وتعالى?”
And:
“Will this genuinely benefit the Ummah?”
Where
Originality
Comes From
Originality comes from understanding two things deeply.
The first is reality.
The founder must understand people, markets, technology, institutions, and the challenges facing society.
The second is Islam.
The founder must understand justice, trust, halal and haram, accountability, mercy, and the purpose of wealth.
When those two worlds meet, something powerful happens.
A Muslim does not merely create a product.
He creates a solution that carries Islamic values naturally within it.
More Builders, Fewer Imitators
The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said:
“Whoever relieves a believer’s distress of the distresses of this world, Allah will relieve him of a distress from the distresses of the Day of Resurrection.”
(Sahih Muslim 2699)
The future of the Ummah will not be rebuilt by better copies.
It will be rebuilt by Muslims who solve real problems in ways that please Allah سبحانه وتعالى.
What problem facing the Ummah today deserves more builders and fewer imitators?
