# Tawakkul Is Not Laziness

As-salamu alaykum
Reza,
Tawakkul Is Not
Laziness
One of the most misunderstood topics in Islam is tawakkul.
Some people think tawakkul means sitting back and “trusting Allah.”
No planning.
No discipline.
No preparation.
But Islam never taught Muslims to be careless.
Accountability Before
Excuses
But Islam teaches accountability before excuses.
When the Muslims lost at Uhud, Allah سبحانه وتعالى did not tell them to only look outward.
Allah سبحانه وتعالى said:
“(What is the matter with you?) When a single disaster smites you, although you smote (your enemies) with one twice as great, you say: ‘From where does this come to us?’ Say (to them), ‘It is from yourselves (because of your evil deeds).’”
(Surah Aal-Imran 3:165)
This is a standard.
Tie
the Camel First
A man asked the Messenger of Allah صلى الله عليه وسلم:
“O Messenger of Allah, should I tie my camel or trust in Allah?”
The Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم replied:
“Tie it and trust in Allah.”
(Sunan At-Tirmidhi 2517, Hasan)
This is the foundation.
Effort comes first. Trust comes with it.
The
Example
of the Birds
The Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم also said:
“If you were to rely upon Allah with the reliance He is due, then He would provide for you just as He provides for the birds: they go out in the morning hungry and return full.”
(Sunan At-Tirmidhi 2344, Sahih)
Notice something powerful.
The birds do not stay in their nests.
They leave early.
They search.
They move.
They put in effort.
That is tawakkul.
The Three Parts of Real
Tawakkul
Real tawakkul has three parts.
First: Put in the effort.
Full effort. Proper planning. No negligence. Tie the camel properly.
Second: Trust Allah سبحانه وتعالى completely.
Not your job.
Not your money.
Not your strategy.
Allah سبحانه وتعالى.
Even if things do not go as expected, you trust that His plan is better than yours.
Third: Be pleased with the outcome.
Sometimes the closed door was protection.
Sometimes the delay was preparation.
Sometimes the hardship was purification.
Tawakkul is not only at the beginning or during the process.
It continues after the outcome becomes clear.
Strength with Calmness
What would happen if Muslims worked relentlessly while remaining completely calm, because their hearts relied on Allah سبحانه وتعالى and not the dunya?
