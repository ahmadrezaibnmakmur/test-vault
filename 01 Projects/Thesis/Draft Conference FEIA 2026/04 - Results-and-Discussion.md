# 4. Results and Discussion

The results are reported following the four operational stages of the PDCA cycle within the broader DMAIC study. Section 4.1 presents the Plan stage through baseline measurement, product stratification, Pareto prioritization, and root-cause diagnosis. Section 4.2 presents the Do stage through CS-engineering corrective-action tracking. Section 4.3 evaluates performance and issue-category movement in the Check stage. Section 4.4 translates the evidence into standardization and continued-control decisions in the Act stage.
