## Discussion

The findings are consistent with Lean Six Sigma customer-support studies that use DMAIC to move from broad service problems to focused process improvements [8]. As in lean call-centre process-improvement studies [6], Pareto analysis prevented the project from treating all inquiries equally. The SaaS context adds a more defensible operational layer: issue-category data, CS-engineering follow-up notes, and post-improvement monitoring by issue category show which recurring problems continued to drive unresolved inquiries.

Practically, SaaS support teams should not rely only on response speed. They need a defect view of unresolved inquiries, a shared tracker between Customer Service and engineering, and a control plan that returns recurring tags to PDCA. This is especially important when unresolved inquiries cause rework, delayed closure, and SLA risk.
