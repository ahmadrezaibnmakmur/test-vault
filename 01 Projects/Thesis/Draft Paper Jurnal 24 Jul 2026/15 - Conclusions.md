# 4. Conclusions

This study shows that Non-FCR in SaaS customer support can be treated as a measurable service-process defect. RQ1 is answered by the baseline and Pareto results: Product 1 was the priority area, and six tags contributed 1,794 defects or 64.42% of Product 1 Non-FCR. RQ2 is answered by the DMAIC results: dominant root causes were concentrated in Machine, Method, and Measurement; PDCA actions were tracked during January-April 2026; and the January-May 2026 early evaluation showed FCR increasing to 73.74%, DPMO decreasing to 262,586, and SLA breach decreasing to 14.56%.

The study is limited by its single-case scope, internal operational data, and short early-evaluation period. Future work should extend the control period, use statistical process control, connect FCR with CSAT or retention outcomes, and validate the approach across additional SaaS products or firms.
