## Control reading

Table 5. Product 1 baseline, PDCA implementation, and early evaluation.

| Period | FCR | Non-FCR | DPMO | SLA breach | Avg. inquiry/month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline 2025 | 67.93% | 2,831 (32.07%) | 320,684 | 20.21% | 735.67 |
| PDCA Jan-Apr 2026 | 72.21% | 881 (27.79%) | 277,918 | 15.39% | 792.50 |
| Early evaluation Jan-May 2026 | 73.74% | 1,064 (26.26%) | 262,586 | 14.56% | 810.40 |

Table 5 answers RQ2 at metric level. FCR increased from 67.93% at baseline to 73.74% in the early evaluation. Non-FCR rate decreased from 32.07% to 26.26%, DPMO decreased from 320,684 to 262,586, and SLA breach decreased from 20.21% to 14.56%. This movement occurred while average monthly issue volume increased from 735.67 to 810.40.

The monthly trend in Fig. 3 shows that 2026 performance improved especially from March to May. Because FCR had already improved near the end of 2025, the result should be interpreted as an evidence-supported early indication rather than a causal proof that every change was produced solely by PDCA.
