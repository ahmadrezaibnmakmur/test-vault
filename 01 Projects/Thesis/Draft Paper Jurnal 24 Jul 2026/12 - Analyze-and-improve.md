## Analyze and improve

Root-cause analysis found that the dominant causes were not primarily agent capacity. Machine, Method, and Measurement were more prominent: report-query logic, journal and settlement logic, queue processes, validation rules, stock calculation, and data mismatches across source transactions and reports.

| PURCHASING | Machine, Method | 63/82 | 75.72% -> 78.99% (+3.27 pp) | Moderate improvement |
| INVENTORY | Machine, Measurement | 50/63 | 73.35% -> 74.83% (+1.48 pp) | Moderate improvement |

Table 3 combines Analyze, Improve, and post-improvement monitoring by issue category. FINANCE and MASTER show the strongest improvement, while ACCOUNTING declines slightly and needs continued monitoring.
