## A B S T R A C T

Software as a Service (SaaS) customer support relies on resolving product-related inquiries at the first interaction, yet recurring issues often create rework, escalation, and delayed closure. This study applies Lean Six Sigma DMAIC to examine and reduce non-first-contact-resolution (Non-FCR) defects in a SaaS live-chat support process. The study uses anonymized operational inquiry records, with 2025 as the baseline, January-April 2026 as the improvement period, and January-May 2026 as the early evaluation period. The analysis combines product-level stratification, Pareto prioritization, Fishbone 6M, PDCA follow-up evidence, and post-improvement monitoring by issue category. Results show that Product 1 was the dominant Non-FCR source, with six issue categories forming the priority improvement area. After PDCA implementation, Product 1 FCR increased from 67.93% to 73.74%, with reductions in DPMO and SLA breach. The findings show that DMAIC can translate SaaS support logs into a focused defect-reduction and control process, although the short evaluation period limits causal claims.
