# 2. Material and method

This research uses a single-case applied process-improvement design based on Lean Six Sigma DMAIC [5]. The case is a SaaS company's live-chat customer support process, and the unit of analysis is one customer inquiry categorized as an issue. Company and product names are anonymized to protect operational confidentiality.

The study uses secondary operational data from customer support records. The baseline period is January-December 2025. Evidence of improvement activities is taken from January-April 2026 CS-engineering follow-up notes, while early performance evaluation uses January-May 2026 Product 1 issue inquiry records.

The main variables are total issue inquiries, inquiries resolved at first contact, inquiries not resolved at first contact, SLA-breach status, product category, issue category, issue pattern, and internal follow-up status. Each issue inquiry is treated as one unit with one defect opportunity.

Table 1. Operational definitions and calculations.

| Metric | Operational definition and calculation |
| --- | --- |
| FCR | Resolved at first contact without follow-up or escalation; FCR rate = FCR inquiries / total issue inquiries. |
| Non-FCR | Not resolved at first contact and requiring additional handling; used as the main service-process defect. |
| DPMO | Defects per million opportunities; Non-FCR / total issue inquiries x 1,000,000. |
| SLA breach | Non-FCR inquiry exceeding the 24-hour resolution limit; used as an impact and control metric. |
| Issue category | Operational category assigned to each issue inquiry; used for Pareto, Analyze, Improve, and Control. |
