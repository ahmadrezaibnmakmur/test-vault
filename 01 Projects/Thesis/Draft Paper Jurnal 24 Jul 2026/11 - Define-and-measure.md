## Define and measure

The process starts when a customer submits an issue through live chat. Customer Service identifies the issue, attempts first-contact resolution, and closes the inquiry when the answer is complete. If the inquiry requires technical validation, manual repair, or additional follow-up, it becomes Non-FCR. SLA breach is treated as an operational impact metric rather than the main defect.

Table 2. Baseline performance by product in 2025.

| Product | Total inquiry | FCR | Non-FCR | FCR rate | SLA breach | SLA breach rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product 1 | 8,828 | 5,997 | 2,831 | 67.93% | 1,784 | 20.21% |
| Product 2 | 30,586 | 28,678 | 1,908 | 93.76% | 1,361 | 4.45% |
| Total | 39,414 | 34,675 | 4,739 | 87.98% | 3,145 | 7.98% |

Table 2 answers RQ1 at product level. Product 1 had far lower FCR than Product 2, with 2,831 Non-FCR defects, 67.93% FCR, and 20.21% SLA breach. Therefore, the next analysis focuses on Product 1.

At issue-tag level, Pareto analysis shows that the largest Product 1 Non-FCR defects were concentrated in six tags, as shown in Fig. 2.

![](assets/figure-02.png)

Fig. 2. Pareto of Product 1 Non-FCR by issue category.

The six largest tags were REPORT ERP, PURCHASING, INVENTORY, ACCOUNTING, FINANCE, and MASTER. Together they contributed 1,794 defects, or 64.42% of Product 1 Non-FCR, making them the focus of Analyze and Improve.
